Coordination compounds have widespread practical applications:

- **Biological Systems:** Haemoglobin ($Fe^{2+}$ complex), Chlorophyll ($Mg^{2+}$ complex), Vitamin $B_{12}$ ($Co^{3+}$ complex).
- **Medicine:** Cisplatin ($cis-[Pt(NH_3)_2Cl_2]$) is an anti-cancer drug. $EDTA$ is used to treat heavy metal poisoning (e.g., lead).
- **Metallurgy:** Extraction of gold and silver via cyanide complexes (MacArthur-Forrest process). Purification of nickel via $[Ni(CO)_4]$ (Mond's process).
- **Analytical Chemistry:** Detection of $Ni^{2+}$ using DMG (rosy-red complex), and $Fe^{3+}$ using $SCN^-$ (blood-red complex).
