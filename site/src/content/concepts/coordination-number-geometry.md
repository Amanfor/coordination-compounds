---
title: Coordination Number & Geometry
summary: The relationship between the number of ligand donor atoms and the spatial
  geometry of the complex.
visual: molecular-geometry
order: 3
---

The **Coordination Number (C.N.)** is the total number of ligand donor atoms directly bonded to the central metal.

- **C.N. = 2:** Linear geometry (e.g., $[Ag(NH_3)_2]^+$).
- **C.N. = 4:** Can be Tetrahedral ($sp^3$) or Square Planar ($dsp^2$).
- **C.N. = 6:** Octahedral geometry, utilizing either inner $d$-orbitals ($d^2sp^3$) or outer $d$-orbitals ($sp^3d^2$).
