---
title: Crystal Field Theory (CFT)
summary: Electrostatic approach explaining $d$-orbital splitting, color, and magnetic
  properties.
visual: d-orbital-splitting
order: 7
---

CFT assumes electrostatic interactions between the metal and ligands, leading to the splitting of degenerate $d$-orbitals.

- **Octahedral Splitting ($\Delta_o$):** $e_g$ set goes up by $+0.6\Delta_o$, $t_{2g}$ set goes down by $-0.4\Delta_o$.
- **Tetrahedral Splitting ($\Delta_t$):** Splitting is inverted ($t_2$ up, $e$ down) and smaller: $\Delta_t = \frac{4}{9}\Delta_o$. Always high-spin.
- **Spectrochemical Series:** Determines ligand splitting power ($I^- < Cl^- < F^- < H_2O < NH_3 < en < CN^- < CO$).
- **High vs Low Spin:** If $\Delta_o < P$ (pairing energy), high-spin complexes form. If $\Delta_o > P$, low-spin complexes form.
- **CFSE:** Crystal Field Stabilization Energy $= [-0.4 \cdot n(t_{2g}) + 0.6 \cdot n(e_g)]\Delta_o$.
- **Colour:** Arises from $d-d$ electron transitions or Ligand-to-Metal Charge Transfer (LMCT).
