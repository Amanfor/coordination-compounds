---
title: Effective Atomic Number (EAN) Rule
summary: Complexes gain stability by achieving a noble gas electron configuration.
visual: ean-calculator
order: 9
---

Sidgwick proposed that complexes are most stable when the central metal acquires the closed-shell electronic configuration of the nearest noble gas (e.g., Kr 36, Xe 54).

- **Formula:** $EAN = Z - O.S. + 2 \times C.N.$
- $Z = $ Atomic number of central metal.
- $O.S. = $ Oxidation state.
- $C.N. = $ Coordination number (or number of ligands $\times$ denticity $\times$ 2 electrons).
- **Examples:** $[Fe(CN)_6]^{4-} \implies 26 - 2 + (2 \times 6) = 36$ (Kr configuration). $[Ni(CO)_4] \implies 28 - 0 + (2 \times 4) = 36$.
