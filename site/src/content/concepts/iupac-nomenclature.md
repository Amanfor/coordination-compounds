Naming rules for coordination entities:

- **Order:** Cation is named first, followed by the anion.
- **Ligand Ordering:** Ligands are named in strictly alphabetical order before the central metal. Multiplicative prefixes (di, tri) do not affect alphabetical order.
- **Anionic Ligands:** End with the suffix '-ido' (e.g., chlorido, cyanido).
- **Neutral Ligands:** Retain their common names with exceptions: $H_2O$ (aqua), $NH_3$ (ammine), $CO$ (carbonyl), $NO$ (nitrosyl).
- **Central Metal:** In anionic complexes, the metal's Latin name takes the suffix '-ate' (e.g., ferrate, cuprate). In cationic/neutral complexes, the standard name is used.
- **Oxidation State:** Designated by Roman numerals in parentheses (e.g., $(II), (III)$).
