**Ligands** are ions or neutral molecules with lone pairs that form coordinate covalent bonds with the central metal.

- **Monodentate:** Single donor atom (e.g., $H_2O, NH_3, Cl^-$).
- **Bidentate:** Two donor atoms (e.g., ethylenediamine $en$, oxalate $ox^{2-}$).
- **Polydentate:** Three or more donor atoms (e.g., $EDTA^{4-}$ is hexadentate).
- **Ambidentate:** Monodentate ligands with two different potential donor atoms (e.g., $NO_2^-, SCN^-, CN^-$).
- **Chelate Effect:** Polydentate ligands forming a closed ring structure (chelate) with the metal ion result in complexes with enhanced thermodynamic stability, primarily driven by a positive entropy change ($\Delta S^\circ > 0$).
