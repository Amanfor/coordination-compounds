Metal carbonyls involve a unique synergic (cooperative) bonding mechanism with $CO$ ligands.

- **$\sigma$-Donation:** $CO$ lone pair donates into an empty metal hybrid orbital ($M \leftarrow C$).
- **$\pi$-Backbonding:** Filled metal $d$-orbitals back-donate into empty $\pi^*$ antibonding orbitals of $CO$ ($M \rightarrow C$).
- **Effects:** Synergic bonding strengthens and shortens the $M-C$ bond but weakens and lengthens the $C-O$ bond, lowering its IR stretching frequency ($\nu_{CO}$).
- **Examples:** $[Fe(CO)_5]$ (trigonal bipyramidal), $[Ni(CO)_4]$ (tetrahedral).
