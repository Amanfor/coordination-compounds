title: Stability of Complexes
summary: Thermodynamic and kinetic factors governing complex formation.

Complex stability is determined by formation constants.

- **Overall Stability Constant ($\beta_n$):** Product of stepwise formation constants ($K_1 \times K_2 \times \dots \times K_n$). Higher $\beta_n$ means greater thermodynamic stability.
- **Charge-to-Radius Ratio:** Higher formal oxidation state and smaller ionic radius enhance stability.
- **Ligand Basicity:** Stronger Lewis bases form more stable complexes.
- **Chelate Effect:** Multidentate ligands form far more stable complexes than monodentate analogues.
- **Irving-Williams Series:** Order of stability for divalent $3d$ metals: $Mn^{2+} < Fe^{2+} < Co^{2+} < Ni^{2+} < Cu^{2+} > Zn^{2+}$.
