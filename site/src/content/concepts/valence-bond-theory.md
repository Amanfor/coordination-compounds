---
title: Valence Bond Theory (VBT)
summary: Explains bonding, geometry, and magnetic behaviour via atomic orbital hybridization.
visual: vbt-hybridization

---

VBT involves the hybridization of metal orbitals to accept ligand electron pairs.

- **$sp^3$ (Tetrahedral):** Occurs with weak field ligands or $d^{10}$ systems.
- **$dsp^2$ (Square Planar):** Strong field ligands force pairing in $d^8$ configurations, vacating an inner $d$-orbital.
- **$d^2sp^3$ (Inner Orbital Octahedral):** Low-spin, spin-paired complexes formed with strong field ligands.
- **$sp^3d^2$ (Outer Orbital Octahedral):** High-spin, spin-free complexes formed with weak field ligands.
- **Magnetic Moment:** $\mu_s = \sqrt{n(n + 2)}$ B.M., where $n$ is the number of unpaired electrons.
