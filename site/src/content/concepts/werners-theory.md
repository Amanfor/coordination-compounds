Alfred Werner's theory explains the fundamental structure of coordination complexes. 

- **Primary Valency (Principal Valency):** Ionisable, non-directional, satisfied by anions, and corresponds to the formal oxidation state of the central metal.
- **Secondary Valency (Auxiliary Valency):** Non-ionisable, highly directional in space (determining geometry), satisfied by neutral molecules or anions, and corresponds to the coordination number of the central metal.
- **Coordination Sphere:** The central metal and directly coordinated ligands enclosed in square brackets $[\dots]$. Species inside do not ionise in solution.
- **Ionisation Sphere:** The ionisable species outside the square brackets that balance the electrical charge.
