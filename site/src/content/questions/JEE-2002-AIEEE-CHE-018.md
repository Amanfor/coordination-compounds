CH3 - Mg - Br is an organometallic compound due to :

---

Compounds that contain at least one carbon metal bond are known as organometallic compounds. In$C{H\_3}  - Mg - Br$ (Grignard's reagent) a bond is present between carbon and$Mg$ (Metal) hence it is an organometallic compound.
