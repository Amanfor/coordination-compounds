The degenerate orbitals of [Cr(H2O)6]3+ are :

---

Degenerate orbitals means orbitals of equal energy.
Cr3+ forms an
octahedral inner orbitals complex and its d orbital get splitted into two differnt energy level.
The two set of degenerate orbitals are
(1) dxy, dyz and
dxz
(2) dx2 - y2 and dz2
