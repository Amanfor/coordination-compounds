Three complexes,
[CoCl(NH3)5]
2+(I),
[Co(NH3)5H2O]3+ (II) and
[Co(NH3)6]
3+(III)
absorb light in the visible region. The correct order of the wavelength of light absorbed by them is :

---

As in a co-ordination compound, the strong field
ligand causes higher splitting of the d-orbitals
Also we know,
strength of ligand$ \propto  {1 \over {{\lambda _{absorbed}}}}$
Order of strength of ligand
NH3 &gt; H2O &gt; Cl-
Therefore decreasing order of wavelength
absorbed is (I) &gt; (II) &gt; (III)
