Which of the following complexes is most stable?

---

1. Stability of a complex increases with chelation.
2. $C_2O_4^{2-}$ (oxalate) is a bidentate chelating ligand.
3. It forms stable 5-membered rings with the central metal ion.
4. The other ligands ($H_2O$, $NH_3$, $Cl^-$) are monodentate and do not form chelate rings.
5. Therefore, $[Fe(C_2O_4)_3]^{3-}$ is the most stable complex.
