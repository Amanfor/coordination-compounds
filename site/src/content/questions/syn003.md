According to Werner's theory, the primary valency and secondary valency of central metal ion in $[Co(NH_3)_5Cl]Cl_2$ respectively are:

---

1. Primary valency corresponds to the oxidation state of the metal ion, which is ionizable.
2. Secondary valency corresponds to the coordination number, which is non-ionizable.
3. In $[Co(NH_3)_5Cl]Cl_2$, the oxidation state of Co is +3 (primary valency = 3).
4. The number of ligands coordinating to Co inside the sphere is $5 (NH_3) + 1 (Cl^-) = 6$ (secondary valency = 6).
5. Option A is correct.
